# Brag Plan: Libraa Group

## What is this app?
Libraa Group (since 2009): ship management, crew management and marine services, working from Indian ports to West Africa. A standalone brand promo, unrelated to the brand film.

## The angle
Modern maritime brand promo: cinematic stock open, the logo lands on the music's first strong bar, a beat-cut montage under the three service words, the clients who trust Libraa, then the tagline end card.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Aerial of a bulk carrier on open sea; narrator: "Every reliable voyage starts long before a ship leaves port." Kinetic caption so it works muted.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Logo reveal on the 3.70 s strong cue: "LIBRAA GROUP / Since 2009".
- Beat-cut montage (7.91 / 10.01 / 11.06 / 12.12 s): "Ship Management", "Crew Management", "Marine Services", "From India to West Africa." - each word on screen as it is spoken.
- "Trusted by": Indian Navy, Adani Ports & SEZ, JSW Group land on their spoken names, then 15 more client logos cascade.

## Outro / punchline
Ship silhouette at sunset, logo, "LIBRAA GROUP", "Ship Management, Crew Management and Marine Services.", libraa.com.

## User flow worth showing
none - company promo.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: modern maritime brand promo
- Interpretation: big type, hard cuts on the beat, slow pushes on footage, restrained.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080, 30 fps
## Duration: 25 s

## Visual identity
- Background: navy #0a0e2a / #12184f; accent mint #8fe0ac, green #159449; text #ffffff
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans 600/700 (the Libraa website font)
- Visuals: stock footage (Pexels/Pixabay) + Libraa logo + client logos. No Libraa photos (user choice).

## Voiceover script
Kokoro-82M via `hyperframes tts`, voice af_heart. Word timings from `hyperframes transcribe` (all words verified).
1. 0.00 s - "Every reliable voyage starts long before a ship leaves port."
2. 3.95 s - "Since 2009, that has been our work at Libraa Group."
3. 8.00 s - "We manage ships. We manage crews. And we deliver marine services, from Indian ports to West Africa."
4. 14.35 s - "Trusted by the Indian Navy, Adani, and JSW."
5. 20.20 s - "Libraa Group. Ship management, crew management, and marine services."

## Audio direction
- Music: bundled happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11 (114.8 BPM), from 0 s; strong cue 3.70 s locks the logo; montage cuts on the beat grid
- Ducked under the voice (sidechain), fade out 23.5-25 s; mix -16 LUFS, -1.5 dB peak
- SFX: none (restraint; nothing over the narrator)
- Audio-reactive: none (helper not installed)

## Storyboard
1. Hook - 0.00-3.70 s - bulk carrier aerial, "Every reliable voyage / starts long before a ship leaves port."
2. Brand - 3.70-7.91 s - logo, LIBRAA GROUP, Since 2009, green rule draws across
3. Services - 7.91-14.22 s - four beat cuts with the service words
4. Clients - 14.22-20.02 s - three hero logos on their names, then the wall
5. End - 20.02-25.00 s - tagline card over sunset silhouette
