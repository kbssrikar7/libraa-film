# Stock B-roll shot list (premium look)

Goal: footage that looks shot for a film, not a slideshow of photos.
Everything here is free (Pexels License or Pixabay Content License, no
attribution required, commercial use allowed). Record every clip used in
CREDITS.md with its page URL.

Rules (from the client):
- Stock only illustrates general lines. Never show a stock ship, yard or
  crew as if it were Libraa's.
- No readable company names, logos, flags of other companies, or faces
  close enough to be identified.
- No ocean sound: download the video only, audio is muted in the film.

## What to look for in every clip

- **Real camera movement**: slow drone push, gimbal walk, slider. Not a static
  tripod shot.
- **4K (3840x2160) or at least 1920x1080, 24/25/30 fps.** 50/60 fps is a bonus:
  it can be slowed down smoothly.
- **Golden hour, blue hour or night** where possible. Flat midday light is
  what makes footage look cheap.
- **8 to 20 seconds long.** We use 2 to 4 seconds of each, so a longer clip
  lets us pick the best moment.
- One colour family across all clips (cool blue/teal water, warm sunlight or
  sodium-orange port lights). Avoid heavily graded or oversaturated clips.

energy the client said is missing.

## How to add them

1. Download the MP4 (the largest HD size is enough; 4K is better).
2. Put it in `public/stock/`, with a short name that says what it shows
   (for example `port-cranes-night.mp4`).
3. Add a line to CREDITS.md: file, source page URL, licence.
