# Brag Plan: Libraa Group

Style reference: the /brag demo videos (github.com/latent-spaces/brag - brag-slim launch video, horse-tinder, taxi-for-taxis). Pure kinetic typography, music + light SFX, no narration, no footage, no ships.

## What is it?
Libraa Group: Ship Management, Crew Management and Marine Services since 2009, from India to West Africa.

## Tone
cinematic / brag-slim-demo style: heavy italic display type, mono kickers, full-bleed colour-block slams on the beat, typed text with cursor, highlight bar, count-up.

## Visual identity
Navy #0a0e2a stage, Libraa green #159449 slams (navy text), mint #8fe0ac accents. Inter Tight 900 italic (display), Inter Tight 600 (body), JetBrains Mono (kickers). Libraa logo + client logos only.

## Storyboard (23 s, 1920x1080, beat grid 114.8 BPM)
1. 0.00-3.70 hook - "every reliable voyage" types in; "starts long / before port." with a green highlight bar
2. 3.70-5.80 green slam on the strong cue - logo, SINCE 2009, LIBRAA GROUP
3. 5.80-9.98 "what we do:" - ship management. / crew management. / marine services. one per two beats, earlier rows dim
4. 9.98-12.07 "where we work:" - from India ... dotted arc ... to West Africa.
5. 12.07-16.25 "trusted by:" - Indian Navy, Adani Ports & SEZ, JSW Group land on beats, then 15 more client logos
6. 16.25-18.34 "since 2009:" - counter 0 -> 17, "years and counting."
7. 18.34-23.00 green end card - logo, LIBRAA GROUP, tagline, libraa.com

## Audio
Bundled happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11; key taps on the typing, soft impacts on the two green slams and the counter, soft drops on the three hero logos. -16 LUFS, fade out 21.6-23 s.
